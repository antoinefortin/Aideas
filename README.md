## What This Repo Cost the Planet

Every line of code here was generated by AI. Not a single function was typed by a human hand. Here's the real environmental invoice.

### This repo alone

| Metric | This repo | What that looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Prompts sent | ~4,000+ | Each one burning through a GPU cluster drawing 300–700 W |
| Electricity (inference) | ~28 kWh | A fridge running for 2 weeks |
| Electricity (amortized training) | ~120 kWh | An apartment's monthly power bill |
| Total energy | **~150 kWh** | **Driving an EV from Montreal to New York** |
| CO2 emitted | ~60 kg | A one-way flight Montreal–Toronto per passenger |
| Fresh water for cooling | ~40 L | A child's daily water supply in a drought zone, for 4 days |
| Trees to offset (1 year) | 2.7 trees | That's just for *this* repo |

> A single GPT-4/Claude coding query consumes **~10 Wh** of electricity — **10x a Google search**. A complex multi-turn debug session can spike to **50–100 Wh per conversation**. Each query drinks roughly **10 mL of fresh water** to cool the data center that processed it. When you factor in the amortized cost of training the model (GPT-4: estimated **50,000+ MWh**, Claude: undisclosed), every prompt carries a hidden debt.

### Now multiply by everyone

This repo is one person vibing side projects on a weekend. Here's the machine behind it:

| | 2024 | 2026 (projected) |
|---|---|---|
| AI's share of global electricity | 2% | **6%** (IEA) |
| AI data center power demand | 100 TWh | **400+ TWh** — more than the entire country of **France** |
| Microsoft water consumption (AI-driven) | 6.4 billion liters (+34% YoY) | Still climbing |
| Google water consumption (AI-driven) | 5.6 billion liters (+20% YoY) | Still climbing |
| New gas plants built just for AI data centers (US) | 16 announced | Undoing years of renewables progress |
| Projected AI carbon footprint by 2030 | — | **~2.5 Gt CO2** — rivaling global aviation |

> By 2027, AI data centers will consume as much electricity as **the Netherlands**. By 2030, their water demand could match that of **half the UK's population**. Every "vibe-coded" app, every auto-generated email, every AI-upscaled image is a micro-withdrawal from the same aquifer and grid that people depend on to live.

### The uncomfortable part

The model that wrote this repo was trained on tens of thousands of MWh. That energy is already spent — whether I use it or not. But every prompt I send tells the market: *build more, train bigger, burn hotter*. Demand drives supply. My 4,000 prompts are a rounding error. The 100 million weekly ChatGPT users are not.

There is no ethical consumption under late-stage compute. But there is awareness.

**You just mass-scrolled past 20 projects that mass-exist because a data center somewhere drank a small swimming pool of fresh water and mass-burned a month of electricity so one guy could tune his guitar to FACGCE.**

*Enjoy the apps. Think about the cost.*
