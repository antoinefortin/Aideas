# Aideas
A place where I just put code of stuff I made with AI

## Projects

- [Tabtone](tabtone/) — One-click Chrome tab audio recorder with local MP3 encoding and automatic filenames.
- [Tabflow](tabflow/) — Égaliseur par onglet, volume booster et effets audio locaux pour Chrome.
- [Footfall](footfall/) — three.js Mixamo walker that leaves vertex-displaced footprints in snow, sand or mud, and scoops, packs and throws snowballs. [▶ Run it](https://antoinefortin.github.io/Aideas/footfall/)
- [Wake](wake/) — three.js walker wading through a GPU-simulated water surface (bow wave, wake, stones, rain) with 12 water presets, from tropical lagoon to liquid mercury. [▶ Run it](https://antoinefortin.github.io/Aideas/wake/)
