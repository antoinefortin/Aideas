# GlitchDrums

Browser-based IDM/glitch drum machine. Upload your own samples, program beats with per-step stutter, pitch-shift, reverse, slice, probability and more. Export as WAV.

## Run it

[Open GlitchDrums](https://antoinefortin.github.io/Aideas/GlitchDrums/)

## Features

- **Sample upload**: drag & drop or browse for WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC — load as many tracks as you want
- **Built-in sounds**: starts with synthesized kick, snare, hihat, clap and rim
- **Step sequencer**: 8, 16, 32 or 64 steps with swing control
- **Per-step editing** (right-click or shift-click a step):
  - Velocity
  - Stutter/retrigger (1x to 32x)
  - Pitch shift (-24 to +24 semitones)
  - Sample start/end (slicing)
  - Reverse playback
  - Probability (0–100%)
  - Decay envelope
  - Pan (L/R)
  - Filter cutoff
- **Master FX**: bitcrusher (depth + rate), distortion, delay (time/feedback/mix), master filter, master volume
- **Random fill**: one-click glitch pattern generator
- **Mute/Solo** per track
- **WAV export**: offline render of the full pattern with all FX
- **Keyboard**: Space to play/stop

## Tips

- Right-click a step to open the step editor without toggling it
- Stutter at 8x–32x with short decay and pitch shift creates classic IDM drill/glitch fills
- Use probability below 100% for evolving patterns that change on every loop
- Sample start control lets you slice into different parts of a longer sample
- Stack the bitcrusher with distortion and delay for total destruction
