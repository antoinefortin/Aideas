A GENTLER APPROACH

A practice, not a performance.

There isn’t one best breathing pattern for everyone. Slow, comfortable breathing can support relaxation. These categories are ways to choose a practice, not proven treatments for separate conditions.

Let the guide be an invitation. Breathe lightly, never as deeply as possible. Shorten the counts or stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy, breathless, or more anxious. Skip holds if uncomfortable.

Breathing exercises aren’t a treatment for persistent anxiety or insomnia. If these keep affecting your life, a healthcare professional can help.

The patterns

Soft landing · 4 in / 6 out: a suggested longer-exhale pace inspired by NHS breathing guidance. Easy rhythm · 5 / 5: gentle equal breathing. Box breathing · 4 / 4 / 4 / 4: an optional structured focus with holds. 4–7–8: an optional wind-down practice, limited here to four cycles. Cyclic sigh · 3 / 1 / 6: inhale, a small second sip, then a relaxed longer exhale. The seconds are a guide, not a studied prescription.

Evidence for relaxation and mood is encouraging; evidence for treating chronic insomnia is limited. No promises of instant sleep.
