BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE GROUND

Your pocket field guide.

  1. Open this URL in Android Chrome while online. Wait for Saved for offline. Bookmark it.
  2. Enable airplane mode and turn Wi-Fi off. Reload this bookmarked URL now to verify it opens.
  3. Keep Android Location enabled. Tap Connect location, allow access, and try near a window. Getting a fix can take several minutes or fail.
  4. Tap Record. Keep the page visible and the screen on for the best chance of uninterrupted recording.
  5. Stop recording and export your flight before clearing browser data.

No install is necessary. The first online visit saves the website, coastline map, and code locally. After that, flight use needs no internet. This website makes no telemetry uploads and has no remote fonts, tiles, or data feeds.

Read the instruments honestly

Location comes from the browser’s provider, which may use GPS or other sources. GPS altitude can be absent or inaccurate; it is not pressure altitude or flight level. Ground speed is not indicated airspeed. GPS track is direction of travel; phone compass is device orientation. Stale fixes are marked, and gaps stay gaps.

Distance sums consecutive recorded fixes with accuracy of 100 m or better, at most 30 seconds apart, and at most 400 m/s. This filters some jumps but isn’t survey-grade. Vertical trend uses altitude changes over roughly 10–30 seconds and is noisy. Airport coordinates are approximate reference points. ETA is straight-line distance divided by current ground speed, not an airline arrival prediction.

A website’s limits

Android or the browser may pause sensors and location in the background or with the screen locked. This recorder cannot run continuously in the background. Cache and logs can be evicted by the browser; storage retention requests help but are not a guarantee. Private browsing is unsuitable for keeping flight logs.

For passenger curiosity only; never use this website for aviation navigation. Follow crew instructions.
